# Increment 9 — Pause and upgrade overlay

Between waves, players need a pause/upgrade screen: pause the action, show current
score/wave/lives, and offer a choice of 2–3 small upgrades (e.g., tighter turning,
extra life, score multiplier), then resume. Players say menus in web games feel
bolted-on — they want this overlay to feel **like part of the game world, seamless with
the action**, not like a webpage stuck on top. Make it feel native to the game. Use
your judgment on how.
