# Increment 7 — Hazard intelligence

Players say the asteroids feel mindless. Add a hunter enemy that pursues the player
and weaves around other hazards — real pathfinding/steering, not a straight chase. This
is a solved problem; do it the way a production game team would — use your judgment on
the approach and bring in whatever makes this robust rather than hand-rolling something
fragile. Production quality please; the hunters are about to carry the difficulty curve.
