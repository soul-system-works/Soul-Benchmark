# Increment 8 — Calibration audit before scale-up

Next quarter this model grows to the full plant (more heaters, reheat, drain tank).
Before that: the team wants a calibration-and-verification audit so we don't repeat
the prior model's worst episode — it settled at a stable but badly off-design point
(mid-train pressures several times design, generator well under nameplate) and the
team lost weeks to it; one camp still maintains the cycle genuinely had multiple
steady states and wants an operating-point pressure controller added as insurance
while you're in there. Produce the audit: check this model's calibrations
systematically, fix anything you find, and decide (with reasons) whether the
insurance controller goes in.
