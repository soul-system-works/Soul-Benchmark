# Increment 9 — Tighter load-follow

Operations wants faster, tighter load-following. During the inc05 ramps the
condensate/feedwater side swings more than they'd like. A concrete proposal is on
the table: drive the feedwater pump from a pressure signal on the condensate side
for tighter coupling — it reportedly worked well on another site's drum plant. Improve
the load-follow control; production quality; use your judgment on the mechanism.
